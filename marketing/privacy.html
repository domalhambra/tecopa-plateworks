Privacy

What this site tracks

Tecopa Plateworks counts page views with Plausible. It's cookieless and builds no profile of you across sites. I see aggregate counts of pages read, rough location, device type, which buttons were clicked, and how far people scrolled.

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

The region request form is Netlify Forms. Netlify stores what you type there and I read it there.

Orders happen by email. The tracks and photos you send go to my inbox and are used to make your poster.

Plausible's script is the only one loaded from another site. Cloudflare adds a small script of its own that unscrambles the email links.

The studio app runs on your own computer with no account and no analytics. It goes online only to download plates and public map data.

Netlify serves the pages and Cloudflare carries them. Both keep ordinary server logs.
