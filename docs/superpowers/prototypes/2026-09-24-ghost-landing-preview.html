Questions

How do I get my tracks out of onX or Gaia?

Both apps let you export GPX in bulk from their websites. If a menu has moved or you get stuck, email me and I'll walk you through it.

Will it look this good on my wall?

You see the exact poster before it prints, at full size, with your tracks and photos on it. If something's off, I fix it. Nothing prints until you say yes.

What happens after I email my tracks?

I build the map around your ground and send you a proof. You ask for changes or say yes, then I send a payment link. Your tracks and photos go to one place: the person who makes your poster.

Where can you map?

Anywhere in the lower 48. Alaska and Hawaii are coming later.

Can I make my own?

Yes. The program that draws these is free, and it's on GitHub. Most people have me do it.

Build your poster

You send me your trips, and I make the poster. It takes a few minutes on your end.

  1. Gather your tracks

    Export your trips from onX, Gaia, Strava or your watch. GPX, KML and KMZ files all work. Send as many as you like, from anywhere in the lower 48.

  2. Pick your photos

    Choose the photos you want on the map: the shed, the summit, the camp. Photos straight from your phone work best, because they remember where they were taken. This step is optional.

  3. Choose a size
