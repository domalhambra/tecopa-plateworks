### How it works

1. Gather your tracks Export your trips from onX, Gaia, Strava or your watch. GPX, KML and KMZ files all work, from anywhere in the lower 48.
2. Pick your photos The shed, the summit, the camp. Photos straight from your phone work best, because they remember where they were taken. This step is optional.
3. Start your order and upload Tell me your name, your email and the title you want on the poster. The next page gives you a link to upload your tracks and photos. Here's what to send.
4. See your sizes I lay your trips out at each size and email you the picture. Pick the size you like and pay by card.
5. See your poster, then say yes I send you a proof before anything prints, with one round of changes. Nothing prints until you say yes. Then it's printed and on its way to your wall.

Button: Start your order

### Add next year's trips

The list isn't finished, and the map doesn't have to be. Send next season's tracks and they join this year's, on the same ground, at the Next year prices below.

- Same ground, same framing. I open your poster where we left it. The new trips are drawn in beside the old ones, and the ones already on the sheet stay as they are.
- It says which edition it is. The caption at the foot of the poster gains Edition 2 and the years it spans, and Edition 3 the year after.
- Your earlier tracks. I delete them 90 days after your poster is delivered. Reply to any of my emails and I'll hold them for next year's poster. If they're already gone, send them again with the new ones.
- Ordering it. Answer Yes, this is a next-year poster on the order form. You get the Next year prices, and you see the new poster before it prints, the same as the first time.

### Questions

**How do I get my tracks out of onX or Gaia?** Both apps let you export GPX in bulk from their websites. If a menu has moved or you get stuck, email me and I'll walk you through it.

**Will it look this good on my wall?** You see the exact poster before it prints, at full size, with your tracks and photos on it. If something's off, I fix it. Nothing prints until you say yes.

**What happens after I send my tracks?** I lay your trips out at each size and email you the picture, with a link to pay for the size you pick. Then I build the map around your ground and send you a proof. One round of changes is included, and until you say yes you can ask for a full refund. Your tracks and photos go to one place: the person who makes your poster.

**How long does it take?** From December to April, your proof comes by email and your poster ships on a Tuesday or Friday. From May to November I'm on a wildland fire crew and out of reach for two to three weeks at a time. Your proof comes when I'm back between assignments, and I can't promise a date.

**Where can you map?** Anywhere in the lower 48. Alaska and Hawaii are coming later.

**Can I make my own?** Yes. The program that draws these is free, and it's on GitHub. Most people have me do it.
